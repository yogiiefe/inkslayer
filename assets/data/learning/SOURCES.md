# Campaign learning references

The 15 contextual Kanji readings and eight katakana vocabulary entries in [campaign.json](../campaign.json) were cross-checked against the references below on 2026-11-14. The glosses are short English labels authored for the game. Japanese-literate human review and learner testing are pending. Each lesson teaches the listed reading in its given sense. Other readings may be correct in other words.

The canonical demonstration uses one standard Japanese stroke sequence per character. See the [KanjiVG template provenance](../kanji/TEMPLATES.md) for the source SVGs, pinned revision, path sampling, and CC BY-SA 3.0 terms. No alternate stroke paths are offered in this curriculum. Shape acceptance and practice coaching are separate.
